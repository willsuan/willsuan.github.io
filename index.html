§ 02 / what I can contribute

Computational work attached to a real research question.

I am not mainly looking to build software for its own sake. I want to contribute computational work that helps answer a real research question, including implementations, validations, and analyses someone in a lab would otherwise have to find time for.

  1. c.01

    Numerical implementation of models

    Translating a paper or sketch into careful, tested code. Small models, clean code paths, tracked assumptions.

  2. c.02

    Model comparison and validation

    Side-by-side fits, identifiability checks, recovery on synthetic data, sensitivity to priors and hyperparameters.

  3. c.03

    Robustness, null models, cross-validation

    Permutation and shuffle controls, finite-sample baselines, repeated splits, the unglamorous part that keeps a result honest.

  4. c.04

    Scalable analysis pipelines

    Tooling that lets a single researcher push a method across many sessions or recordings without losing reproducibility.

  5. c.05

    Simulation & computational experiments

    Generating ground-truth data, running parameter sweeps, designing tests that isolate one effect at a time.

  6. c.06

    ML training & evaluation

    Training loops with attention to correctness and efficiency, careful evaluation, and experiments that say something specific.

  7. c.07

    Visualization of high-dimensional dynamics

    Trajectories, embeddings, manifold structure, geometry. Figures meant to support a claim, not decorate it.

§ 04 / current project direction in progress

Latent-variable and representation-geometry benchmarks for public neural data.

I am exploring a small reproducible benchmark for comparing latent-variable methods and representation-geometry metrics on public neural population datasets. The goal is to make it easier to test methods under finite-sample noise, compare baselines, and produce clear reproducible figures.

  • PCA / FA / GPFA-style baselines
  • CEBRA or related embeddings
  • RSA · CKA · dimensionality metrics
  • finite-sample robustness
  • public neural datasets
  • reproducible figures
